# Island Forge Leadership Syllabi  
**Principal & Senior Principal / Partner Tracks – Version 1.1**  
_Date: 1 May 2025_

These programs transform seasoned technical leaders into owners of strategy, finance and culture—essential preparation for partnership buy-in.

---

## Section 5 — Principal Leadership Mastery (12 Weeks)

**Objective:** Equip Principals to own multi-million-dollar portfolios, craft 3-year technology roadmaps, and steer sales negotiations at C-suite level.

**Format:** Eight weeks of masterclasses & labs, three weeks running a \$2-3 M book of business, one week strategic plan defence.

| Week | Key Focus                     | Core Objectives                                                                       | Required Deliverables                                   |
|------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | **Annual Budgeting**          | Draft BU budget incl. payroll ladder, infra lease, grant income                       | Annotated FY budget + cash-flow forecast                |
| 2    | **Advanced FinOps**           | Unit-economics for GPU / cloud vs. colo; break-even & IRR models                      | CapEx vs. OpEx decision memo                            |
| 3    | **Enterprise Sales**          | Win-themes, elevator pitches, competitor teardown                                     | Two-page battle-card; 5-min recorded pitch              |
| 4    | **High-Stakes Negotiation**   | Contract T&Cs, indemnity caps, escalation clauses                                     | Red-lined MSA section + concession tracker              |
| 5    | **Strategic Account Growth**  | Land-and-expand playbooks • Joint Success Plans (JSP)                                 | JSP template populated for a live client                |
| 6    | **People & Succession**       | Create succession matrix • Delegate 30 % workload • Conflict-resolution simulations   | Succession plan + delegation audit                      |
| 7    | **Governance & Equity**       | Option pool mechanics • Valuation & dilution • Partner buy-in tranches                | Cap-table scenario sheet                                |
| 8    | **Thought Leadership**        | Conference CFP writing • Tech blogging • Webinar hosting                              | Published article or accepted CFP                       |
| 9-11 | **Live Portfolio Command**    | Lead \$2-3 M portfolio • Maintain ≥ 55 % margin • Weekly board updates                | Weekly margin + NPS dashboards                          |
| 12   | **Capstone Defence**          | Present 3-year strategic plan & risk register to Partner Board                        | Board score ≥ 80 % • **Principal Badge**                |

## Section 6 — Senior Principal / Partner Program (16 Weeks + Ongoing)

**Objective:** Cultivate firm owners who steer strategy, governance and brand while mentoring the next generation of leaders.

**Format:** Four months of partner-level projects, quarterly council presentations, ongoing governance duties.

| Phase | Duration | Core Activities                                                                           | Key Deliverables / KPIs                                 |
|-------|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| **A – Orientation** | Weeks 1-2 | Deep-dive into firm P&L, balance sheet, risk register                        | Sign Partner Nondisclosure & Capital Contribution docs  |
| **B – Growth Council** | Weeks 3-6 | Chair “Top-10 Clients” council • Approve pricing strategies                | Council minutes • Signed pricing approvals              |
| **C – Equity & Capital** | Weeks 7-10 | Lead quarterly Partner Numbers Day • Decide dividend vs. reinvestment      | Dividend policy vote • Capital call memo (if any)       |
| **D – Strategic Initiative** | Weeks 11-14 | Sponsor firm-wide initiative (e.g., micro-DC solar build, new SaaS spin-out) | Initiative charter • Stage-gate report                  |
| **E – Partner Off-Site** | Week 15 | Facilitate annual off-site; ratify 3-year strategic plan                   | Approved strategic plan; OKR set                        |
| **F – Governance (ongoing)** | … | Board meetings, audit sign-off, M&A reviews, partner admissions            | Board minutes • Audit letter • New partner onboarding   |

### Assessment & Vesting

| Level               | Passing Criteria (rolling)                                  | Equity / Profit-Share           |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Principal**       | Board score ≥ 80 % + Portfolio margin ≥ 55 %                | **0.5-1.0 ×** salary buy-in → Class B units (4-yr vest) |
| **Senior Principal** | Unanimous partner vote + Firm-wide initiative success KPI   | Additional **0.5 ×** salary buy-in; Class A units; full voting rights |

- Equity financed via payroll deduction over 3 years (non-recourse).  
- Partners attend **Quarterly Numbers Day**; dividends declared by majority vote.  
- Failure to meet KPIs triggers remediation plan; two consecutive misses lead to equity-buyback at book value.
