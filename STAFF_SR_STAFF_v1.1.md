# Island Forge Leadership Syllabi  
**Staff & Senior Staff Cohorts – Version 1.1**  
_Date: 1 May 2025_

These tracks pick up where the Mid-Level Accelerator ends, focusing on business acumen, client growth, and people leadership. Graduates feed the **Principal** pipeline.

---

## Section 3 — Staff Leadership Launchpad (8 Weeks)

**Objective:** Equip engineers to manage single-project P&Ls, mentor juniors, and farm existing accounts for growth.

**Format:** Six weeks of workshops + labs, two weeks running a real client mini-portfolio with senior oversight.

| Week | Key Focus                  | Core Objectives                                                                        | Required Deliverables                                      |
|------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | **P&L Basics**             | Read live project P&L • Build a zero-based sprint budget • Margin vs. utilisation      | Annotated P&L explaining each cost line                    |
| 2    | **Advanced Agile**         | Run backlog grooming • Write Definition-of-Done matrix • Velocity forecasting          | Sprint plan + forecast accuracy ≤ ±10 %                    |
| 3    | **Account Farming 101**    | Stakeholder mapping • QBR structure • Upsell heuristics                                | 10-slide QBR deck for an existing Island Forge client      |
| 4    | **People Management**      | 1-on-1 coaching • Feedback models (SBI/SAR) • Diversity & inclusion primer             | Two written 1-on-1 summaries • Inclusion improvement idea |
| 5    | **Compliance & Risk**      | SOC-2 control walkthrough • Draft incident report                                      | Completed mock incident report (SEV-2)                     |
| 6    | **Bid & Proposal Writing** | RFP vs. sole-source • Value pricing • Discount bands                                   | Two-page capability statement + pricing grid               |
| 7    | **Capstone Prep**          | Build 6-month staffing & margin plan for a single account                              | Draft capstone plan (peer review)                          |
| 8    | **Capstone & Panel Demo**  | Present plan to panel • Respond to budget challenges                                   | Panel pass/fail • **Staff Badge**                          |

## Section 4 — Senior Staff Strategic Program (10 Weeks)

**Objective:** Develop multi-account portfolio managers who can drive commercial growth and mentor Staff engineers.

**Format:** Six weeks of masterclasses, three weeks leading a \$1 M book of business, one week of strategic defence.

| Week | Key Focus                   | Core Objectives                                                                     | Required Deliverables                                   |
|------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | **Portfolio Roll-ups**      | Consolidate multi-project budgets • Identify margin leaks                           | Portfolio health dashboard (Looker / PowerBI)           |
| 2    | **Scenario Planning**       | 10 % rate cut vs. 20 % bench simulations • Contingency planning                      | Mitigation plan & sensitivity chart                     |
| 3    | **Advanced Account Growth** | Create 12-month expansion roadmap • Develop SOW change-orders                       | Roadmap deck + draft SOW (change request)               |
| 4    | **Negotiation & Contract**  | Master Services Agreement clauses • Rate-card strategies                            | Red-lined MSA excerpt + negotiation notes               |
| 5    | **Mentorship at Scale**     | Coach two Staff engineers through promotion rubric • Set OKRs                       | Promotion dossier drafts                                |
| 6    | **Compliance Deep-Dive**    | HIPAA / GDPR DPIA leadership • IP assignment risk matrix                            | Completed DPIA checklist                                |
| 7-9  | **Live Portfolio Leadership** | Run \$1 M client cluster • Weekly margin/NPS reports                                | ≥ 55 % gross margin • Weekly NPS >= 8.5                 |
| 10   | **Capstone Defence**        | Present growth roadmap to exec board • Live Q&A on risk and finance                 | **Senior Staff Badge**                                  |

## Assessment & Promotion

| Level        | Passing Criteria                                 | Next-Step Eligibility                       |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **Staff**    | 360° peer review ≥ 4.0 / 5 + Capstone pass       | Eligible for **Senior Staff** track after 6 months billable |
| **Senior Staff** | Board panel score ≥ 80 % + Portfolio margin ≥ 55 % | Invitation to **Principal** cohort after 9 months |

- Two re-take opportunities per assessment (within 45 days).  
- Badges issued via GitHub Actions → Credly.  
- Promotion requires both badge and utilisation targets met.
